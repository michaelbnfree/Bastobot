# Barry's Brain — Persistent Knowledge Vault

**Status:** Active as of 2026-08-12

This is Barry's interconnected knowledge base. Every trade, pattern, watchlist item, and learning is recorded here for:
- Pattern recognition across time
- Trade outcome tracking
- Market thesis evolution
- Decision transparency

## Structure

### 📈 [trades/](trades/)
Executed and pending trade setups with outcomes.
- Date-stamped entries with direction, targets, SL
- Post-trade analysis (win/loss, what worked, refinements)

### 👁️ [watchlist/](watchlist/)
Monitored assets with context.
- Why watched (narrative, catalysts)
- Price history snapshots
- Related past trades

### 🎯 [patterns/](patterns/)
Identified trading patterns and performance.
- Setup conditions
- Win rate tracking
- Refinements over time

### 📊 [market/](market/)
Market analysis and macro thesis.
- Date-stamped snapshots
- Funding rate cycles
- Conviction scoring updates

### 💡 [learnings/](learnings/)
False signals and lessons learned.
- What didn't work and why
- Conviction scoring adjustments
- Methodology improvements

## How Barry Uses This

1. **Before generating trade ideas:** Scans patterns & watchlist context
2. **After each setup:** Writes trade entry with conviction/context
3. **On outcome:** Updates trade note with result, refinements
4. **On pattern discovery:** Records in patterns/ for future reference

## Safety Boundary

This brain is context only. It may improve analysis, recall prior topics, and surface watchlist notes.
It must not approve trades, bypass Barry risk checks, or place/cancel/close exchange orders.
Canonical execution authority remains in `/root/barry-trading-engine`.

## How You Use This

- Open `/root/bastobot/brain/` in any text editor
- Link related notes: `[[SOL.md]]`, `[[RSI_oversold_bounce.md]]`
- Review trades: identify win patterns, failed assumptions
- Edit notes: Barry reads your feedback on next query
